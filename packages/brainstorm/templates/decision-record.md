## Context and goal

Describe the actual situation and decision. A useful component or an interesting response does not accept a whole option.

## Candidate Idea IDs

Link the complete options and retained / parked alternatives. One viable direction is allowed; do not invent alternatives to fill a quota.

## Criteria and tradeoffs

State the criteria that change this choice, including hard constraints and conflicts between components. Use weights, anchors and sensitivity checks only when they help; do not invent numerical precision.

## Evidence, inference, and unknowns

Distinguish user response, observed facts, external sources and model inference. Missing evidence remains unknown.

## Decision

Record the actual decision and its authorized scope. Agent recommendations remain proposals until the required decision maker accepts them.

## Consequences and risks

Include scope exclusions and what the decision does not establish.

## Open questions and revisit triggers

Name what would change the choice. Reference the next action and pause boundary in Current Continuation; do not create a second execution-state authority.
